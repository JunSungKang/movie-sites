# Code of Conduct

MovieSites welcomes contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels. Participation in issues, pull requests and other project spaces should be respectful, constructive and focused on improving the project.

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Be patient with questions, language differences and unfamiliarity with the tools.
- Respect privacy and intellectual property. Do not post personal information, private media or credentials without permission.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, sexualize interactions or deliberately disrupt discussions.
- Accept reasonable moderation and give others room to correct mistakes.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content, close disruptive discussions, warn participants or restrict participation using the repository's moderation facilities. Responses should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

For a sensitive conduct report, contact a maintainer through an available private channel. If none is available, open an issue asking only how to arrange private contact; do not publish incident details, names or private information there. A dedicated conduct-reporting address has not been established. Participants should avoid retaliation against people raising concerns in good faith.
